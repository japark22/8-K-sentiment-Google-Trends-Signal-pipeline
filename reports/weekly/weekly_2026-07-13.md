## 3. Known Limitations (reminder)

Per `PIPELINE_NOTES.md`:

- **Survivorship bias.** The universe is expanded toward *today's* S&P 500
  membership (public Wikipedia list), so companies that left the index are
  underrepresented — a known, documented approximation.
- **Baseline sentiment lexicon in use.** `data/lm_master_dictionary.csv` is
  **not present**, so sentiment is computed with the transparent baseline word
  list, not the full Loughran-McDonald dictionary. Results are illustrative;
  drop in the full LM CSV for research-grade tone scores.
- **Google Trends is relative and revision-prone.** Values are rescaled by
  Google and can be revised; the factor is treated as a coarse, lagged
  (closed-week-only) feature.
