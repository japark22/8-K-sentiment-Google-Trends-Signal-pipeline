## 3. Known Limitations (reminder — see `PIPELINE_NOTES.md`)

- **Survivorship bias:** the universe is built from the *current* index membership (public Wikipedia list), so companies that were delisted or removed are absent. Historical results are biased upward relative to a true point-in-time universe.
- **Sentiment lexicon:** the full Loughran-McDonald dictionary was **not found on disk**, so the pipeline is running on the transparent **baseline** placeholder word list. It is a labeled placeholder, **not** production-grade financial sentiment — treat tone values and the near-zero IC accordingly.
- **Google Trends is relative and revision-prone:** values are index-normalized (0–100), rescale over time, and can be revised, so week-over-week factors are approximate and only values knowable in that week are used.
- **Prices** come from `yfinance` (Yahoo Finance) and can be re-scaled/adjusted over time.
