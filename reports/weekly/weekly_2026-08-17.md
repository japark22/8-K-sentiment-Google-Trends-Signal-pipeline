## 3. Known Limitations (see `PIPELINE_NOTES.md`)

- **Survivorship bias:** the universe is built from *today's* S&P 500 membership (public Wikipedia list), so names delisted or removed during the sample are absent. Backtested results are biased upward versus a true point-in-time universe.
- **Sentiment lexicon:** `data/lm_master_dictionary.csv` is **not present** on disk, so scoring uses the clearly-labelled **baseline** placeholder word list, not the full Loughran-McDonald dictionary. See the saturation diagnostic above.
- **Google Trends is relative and revision-prone:** values are index-normalized (0–100), rescale as history extends, and can be revised. Only closed weeks (W-2 → W-1 change) inform the factor.
- **Prices** come from Yahoo Finance via `yfinance` and are subject to retroactive adjustment.
